1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website stores in your browser. Similar technologies, such as localStorage, work in the same way and are covered by this policy.

2. What this site currently uses

At the moment this website stores only one item, and it is strictly necessary for the site to work as you asked it to:

  • aidentiq_theme — remembers whether you chose the light or dark version of the page. Stored in your browser via localStorage, never sent to us or to anyone else, kept until you clear your browser data.

No analytics, advertising or tracking cookies are set. Because the only stored item is strictly necessary, no consent banner is required for it under the ePrivacy Directive.

3. Third-party requests

The page currently loads its typefaces from Google Fonts, which means your browser sends a request to Google servers and your IP address becomes visible to Google. [To be confirmed by the lawyer: several EU decisions treat this as a transfer requiring consent — self-hosting the fonts removes the issue entirely.]

4. If analytics is added later

Before any analytics or marketing tool is switched on, a consent banner must be added, the tool must stay blocked until consent is given, and the table above must be extended with the name, purpose, provider and lifetime of every cookie it sets.

5. How to control cookies

You can delete stored data and block cookies in your browser settings. Blocking the item listed above only means the site will not remember your colour theme.
